## Project Overview

This dashboard is designed to help users explore sales and profitability through interactive visual analysis.

### Dashboard components

- **Executive KPIs:** Total Sales, Total Profit, and Profit Margin
- **Top N analysis:** Dynamic ranking of sub-categories by sales and profitability
- **Monthly trend analysis:** Sales and profit over time
- **Geographic analysis:** State-level sales and profitability
- **Interactive filters:** Category and Region

### Questions explored

- Which sub-categories contribute the most sales?
- How do sales and profit change over time?
- Which states and regions perform strongly or weakly?
- How do results differ by category and region?
